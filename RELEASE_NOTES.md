**Bo1ZPocket 0.3.46:** download **Bo1ZPocket.ipa** below and install it over the app you have.

- Shooting feels instant: blood shows the frame you fire, gibs and deaths land a tick sooner, and the muzzle flash no longer stops after ~120 shots.
- Zombies die with BO1's own varied death animations, and bodies disappear a few seconds after they fall.
- Going down no longer makes the zombie on top of you spaz out; with Quick Revive they walk off as in BO1.
